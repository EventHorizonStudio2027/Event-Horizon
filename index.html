Fate’s Passage is built around choices that visibly change who you are and how the world treats you.

Three paths, one destiny

Choose three specializations, then two Minors and a Major. Your class name evolves with every choice.

Your body tells the story

Hair, skin, eyes, markings and sometimes horns, wings or fangs change across 11 appearance stages.

A focused skill bar

Learn up to 14 skills, but only 8 are active at once, plus a dedicated slot for your Ultimate.

The Triune gift

A rare talent that fuses three specializations into one of 120 Triunes. The Triuned are admired, envied and feared.

Ten astrology signs

Pick one at creation and keep it. Each sign grants three passive bonuses.

Ten damage types

Every enemy attack has a type. Your resistances decide how much of it lands.

Twenty mercenaries

Recruit companions who each have their own story, personal quest and bonded skill.

A home from day one

Every Fatewalker starts with a small home in their starter capital.

It doesn’t end at 50

Raids, Mythic Dungeons, PvP and Triune Arenas, World Bosses, Legendary Crafting, Skill Melding, Guild Wars and seasonal events.
